Who We Are
About CyberNova
🛡️

CyberNova was founded on a singular principle: in an age where digital threats evolve daily, your security posture must evolve faster. We are a team of elite cybersecurity professionals, former intelligence analysts, and seasoned engineers united by a common mission.

From penetration testing to enterprise security architecture, we deliver measurable outcomes — not just reports. Our methodology combines cutting-edge offensive security techniques with proven defensive frameworks to give our clients genuine protection.

We operate across multiple sectors including finance, healthcare, government, and critical infrastructure, bringing sector-specific threat knowledge to every engagement.

Certified ethical hackers and security architects
ISO 27001 and POPIA compliance expertise
Proprietary threat intelligence platform
